    How to play

    • • The goal is to clear the whole lot — get all 20 buses off the grid.
    • • Each bus is locked to one direction (◀ ▶ ▼) based on how it's parked.
    • • Tap a bus: if every cell in its arrow direction is empty, it drives itself all the way to the bus stop.
    • • A blocked bus (red arrow) can't move — clear the buses in front of it first.
    • • Bigger buses take up more space but carry more passengers.
    • • Everyone waits in one shared, mixed-color queue — don't let it overflow!
