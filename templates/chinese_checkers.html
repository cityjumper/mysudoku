How many players?

Chinese Checkers is played with 2, 3 or 6 players, each taking a point of the star.

Players

Rules

  • • Each player starts with 10 pieces in their colored point of the star.
  • • On your turn, move one piece a single step into an adjacent empty hole...
  • • ...or jump it over any pieces (yours or an opponent's) in a chain, landing in empty holes.
  • • You can't mix a step and a jump in the same turn.
  • • The dashed ring marks your target point — be first to fill it with all 10 pieces to win!
