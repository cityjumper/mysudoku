Some numbers are already filled in — solve the rest so every row and column checks out.

How to Play

Every full row and column (skipping the black squares) reads as one equation: number, operator, number, equals sign, result. Grey squares are given — fill in the white squares so all six equations check out, and there's only one way to do it.

Controls

Click a square and type a number. Use the arrow keys to jump between squares. The +, −, ×, ÷ and = signs are fixed clues.
