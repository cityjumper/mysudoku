How to play

  • • Every square is one corner (¼) of a picture — top-left, top-right, bottom-left or bottom-right.
  • • Tap any two pieces to swap them — anywhere on the board, matching or not, as often as you like.
  • • A green edge glows when two pieces are correctly placed next to each other.
  • • Get all 4 corners of a picture into the right arrangement and they click together.
  • • Completed pictures disappear and new pieces drop in from above to fill the gap.
  • • On Easy, each piece's outer corner is softened — a shape cue for which corner it is. Hard removes that cue.
  • • Stuck? Tap "Hint" to highlight a swap that completes a picture, or "Shuffle" to mix up the board.
