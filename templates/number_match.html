How to play

  • • Tap two numbers that are equal or add up to 10.
  • • They must share a straight line — row, column, or diagonal — with no other number between them.
  • • The last number of a row and the first number of the next row also count as connected.
  • • Matched pairs vanish and add to your score.
  • • Clear the whole board to advance to the next (bigger) stage.
  • • Not sure what to play? Tap "Hint" to highlight a valid pair.
  • • Stuck with no valid pair? Tap "Add Row" — it copies the remaining numbers into fresh rows at the bottom.
